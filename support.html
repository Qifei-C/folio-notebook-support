Common questions

How do I open a notebook?

On iPhone Duo, unfold the phone: the cover on the outer screen opens onto its pages. On other iPhones, Folio opens straight to your notebook.

Why do I see one page sometimes and two pages other times?

When the inner screen is wide, Folio shows two pages side by side. When it's tall, it shows one.

How do I see the whole notebook?

Swipe up on the pages with two fingers, or tap the book button. Pick Flip, Strip or Grid in the gear's menu. Swipe down with two fingers, or tap a page, to go back to writing.

How do bookmarks work?

Tap Bookmark once to mark the right-hand page, or twice quickly for the left-hand page. Tap it again on a marked page to remove the ribbon. In one-page view it marks the page you see.

Why doesn't search find a word I just wrote?

Folio reads a page a few seconds after you stop writing on it. Search reads handwriting mainly in English, and it doesn't search words inside photos, pictures or PDFs.

Where are my notebooks kept? How do I back them up?

On your iPhone only. They're included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup. Deleting Folio deletes them.

How do I move, resize or delete a picture or text box?

Touch and hold it with a finger.
