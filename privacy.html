Folio Notebook

Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Folio Notebook ("Folio") is a handwriting notebook for iPhone. This policy says what happens to the things you put in it. The short version: Folio doesn't collect anything, and your notebooks never leave your phone.

What Folio stores

Your notebooks (their pages, ink, typed text, pictures, bookmarks, covers and settings) are saved in Folio's own storage on your iPhone. Pictures you add from Photos or Files are copied into that storage. Folio keeps what its search has read of your handwriting there too, so it doesn't have to read it again.

What Folio sends

Nothing. Folio has no account and no sign-in, and it doesn't connect to the internet. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking code, and no code from anyone but Apple.

Photos and Files

Folio uses the system's own pickers. It sees only the pictures and files you choose, and never your whole photo library.

Handwriting search

Folio reads your handwriting with Apple's PencilKit on your iPhone, so your search can find it.

Cover pictures made with Image Playground

On phones that support it, you can make a cover with Apple's Image Playground. Apple's privacy policy covers that feature.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, your notebooks are included in that backup like any other app's data. Apple's privacy policy covers backups. Deleting Folio deletes its notebooks from your phone.

Children

Folio collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
